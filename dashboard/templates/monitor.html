Evaluation and limitations

This build evaluates its anomaly rules and Isolation Forest on deterministic synthetic traffic. Detection on synthetic patterns is easier than on real intrusions and is not a production detection guarantee. The encrypted traffic classifier's older labeled dataset has no session identifiers, so its historical 62.7% accuracy / 0.632 macro F1 is a random flow split and may leak capture-specific patterns. New capture runs now record a session ID; the classifier report uses whole-session holdout when at least two sessions exist.

No payloads are read. Rules can miss attacks that do not match their thresholds and can alert on benign bursts, backups, or unusual applications. Use findings to prioritize human review.
